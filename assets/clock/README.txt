dial.png — the analog watch face rendered inside the bezel slot.

Provenance: copied verbatim from ../ApexHUD-Web/shared/assets/dial.png
(the legacy session-bar centerpiece dial) during Port 01. Used under the
project's personal-use exemption (see DECISIONS.md). The image is square;
the renderer clips it to a circle and cover-fits it into the bezel.

Original ApexHUD-Web note: "Drop your own round image here as 'dial.png'
to fill the bezel slot. Any square PNG works; round/circular subjects look
best since the slot is clipped to a circle."
